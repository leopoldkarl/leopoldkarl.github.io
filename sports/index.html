Sports

Alongside being a mathematician, I consider myself an athlete. Throughout my life I always engaged in sports and my parents always kindly supported my sport activities. As a child I used to play Baseball, do gymnastics, and gave both judo and soccer a fairly brief try. In my teens I took up sailing and completely fell for it. For five years, I climed the National leaderboards, until my crew and I were part of the Austrian Youth National Team of the Austrian Sailing Federation for my last two years in the sport, competed at multiple World and European Championships with some solid results, and won both the Austrian Nationals and the Austrian Youth Championships. For multiple reasons, I stopped sailing to head to Zurich and take up my studies of mathematics. During that time I found a new love for endurance sports, took up running, then cycling, and finally swimming. In 2024, I started to compete again, this time in triathlon. And well, pursuing this sport is an ongoing endeavour.

Personal bests 2026 2025 2024

A brief Timeline of my Achievements

  • 2026 — won the Triathlon Langau and the Triathlon Challenge Allentsteig; took my age group at IRONMAN 70.3 Hradec Králové; finished 9th in M18–24 at the IRONMAN 70.3 World Championship in Nice; improved to 3:46:15 over the middle distance in Podersdorf and to 1:57:53 over the olympic distance.
  • 2025 — won the Allentsteig Triathlon; broke two hours over the olympic distance twice (1:59:21 in Uster, 1:59:51 in Zürich); took my age group at the 5-Tage-Berglaufcup; finished the full Traunsee Bergmarathon over 63 km and 4500 hm; improved my marathon to 2:37:20 and my half marathon to 1:14:00.
  • 2024 — first season of racing: first triathlon in April, first win in August at the Uster Triathlon, 3rd on the Fural half distance of the Traunsee Bergmarathon, a win with a team of two at the SOLA Duo, and a marathon debut in 2:44.
