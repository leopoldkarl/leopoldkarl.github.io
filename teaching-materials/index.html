Linear Algebra

Lecture notes

  • Lineare Algebra I & II — my summary (in German) of Prof. Richard Pink's course at ETH Zurich (HS 2021 / FS 2022), opening with a handwritten overview of the main results of each chapter.

Books

  • K. Jänich, Lineare Algebra — where to start. Short chapters, more motivation than machinery, and test questions at the end of each chapter; in German.
  • S. Roman, Advanced Linear Algebra — for a second pass. It begins roughly where a first course ends and treats modules, tensor products and infinite-dimensional spaces.

Further resources

  • P. R. Halmos, Linear Algebra Problem Book — the whole theory told as problems with hints and solutions. The fastest way to find out whether you really understood a definition.
