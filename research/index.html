Research

I'm at the start of my research career, so there's not too much to find here yet. However, I try to make up for this by adding some cute fotos on this page.

The two topics I am currently interested in are the strength of compactness statements of relational structures in ZF (see Logical compactness and constraint satisfaction problems for an introduction or The CSP Dichotomy, the Axiom of Choice, and Cyclic Polymorphisms for one of the main results already established on that topic) and automatic continuity for projective clone homomorphisms, which I work on together with my PhD supervisor, Michael Pinsker; our first result on it is the preprint listed below.
